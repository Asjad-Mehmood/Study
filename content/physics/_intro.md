These notes follow the new PECTAA Physics 12 book (National Curriculum 2023, session 2026-27), chapter by chapter in the book's order. The book lists chapters 1 to 9 in its contents, but numbers them 13 to 21 inside (continuing from Physics 11), so headings here show both.

Every chapter has the same parts, so you always know where to look:

1. **Key ideas**: the chapter explained in simple language, topic by topic.
2. **Formulas sheet**: every equation with its symbols and SI units.
3. **Derivations**: the important ones written step by step, as asked in long questions.
4. **MCQs with answers**: the book's exercise MCQs solved, plus extra practice MCQs.
5. **Short questions**: the book's short/conceptual questions answered in 2–4 lines.
6. **Long questions**: likely long questions with points to cover.
7. **Numericals solved**: every end-of-chapter problem worked out with given, formula, substitution and answer.
